Track: Finance (F1) — The Irregular Income Problem

Problem Statement: People with irregular income (gig workers, delivery riders, freelancers, vendors) run out of money in lean months even though their annual income would cover their needs. Existing budgeting tools assume fixed monthly salary and fail this group, forcing them to borrow at high interest or sell assets during slow months.

Idea Description: Finance Guru is a mobile-first financial app for people with irregular income. It uses income smoothing — in good months, it suggests saving into two buffers: a Lean Month Fund (covers seasonal drops) and an Emergency Fund (unexpected costs like medical bills or repairs). When a lean month hits, Finance Guru activates the buffer and shows a daily safe spending limit — zero borrowing, zero interest. Tracks fixed expenses, shows 12-month income trends, and predicts lean months. A delivery rider saving 15% in good months can cover 2 lean months fully — saving ₹16,000+ per year.
